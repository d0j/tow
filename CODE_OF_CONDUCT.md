# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and publishing other people's private information are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@d0j](https://github.com/d0j). Reports are handled
confidentially; the maintainer may remove content and restrict participation as the Covenant's enforcement
guidelines describe.
